Our Core Philosophy

For too long, the people have been fed empty promises—like momos with too much dough and no filling. The MJP promises a government that is fully stuffed with action, transparency, and flavor. We stand for Equality, Steamed Progress, and Spicy Justice.

Key Pledges & Policies

🥢

Universal Basic Momo (UBM)

No citizen should go to bed hungry. Every citizen will be guaranteed one plate of steaming hot momos per day. UBM will be the cornerstone of our poverty eradication program.

🌶️

The Chutney Regulation Act

We will establish a central regulatory body to ensure red chili chutney maintains a standardized level of spice. Zero Tolerance for Mayonnaise: Strict penalties for vendors substituting authentic chutney with mayo.

🚶‍♂️

Steaming Corridors

Dedicated pedestrian pathways across major cities, lined with certified momo vendors, ensuring you are never more than 500 meters away from a fresh plate of momos.

🎉

Fried Momo Weekends

While we advocate for steamed progress on weekdays, weekends will be officially designated for Fried, Tandoori, and Kurkure Momos, subsidized by the state to promote national happiness.

A Message to the Citizens

Whether you prefer vegetarian, chicken, pork, or paneer momos, you are equal in the eyes of the MJP. We only draw the line at chocolate momos, which will be subject to a national referendum.
